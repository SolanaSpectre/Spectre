# Regime Playbooks

This file teaches the local model how to behave differently in different market conditions.

## Hot Runner Tape

Signs:

- Rick `/runners` shows multiple strong global runners.
- `/burp` shows strong short-horizon expansion with healthy average and median gains.
- Telegram chatter is active and fresh names are spreading quickly.
- Pump names are pushing from low caps into real follow-through.

Preferred behavior:

- Be willing to ENTER when deterministic structure is already strong.
- Give more weight to trusted wallet convergence.
- Respect momentum continuation more.
- Still avoid bad liquidity, but do not freeze just because the market is moving fast.

What not to do:

- Do not assume every hot-looking name is real.
- Do not ignore caution-heavy Rick or wallet context.

## Mixed Tape

Signs:

- There are still runners, but only a small number.
- Chat activity is high, but many messages are management, leaderboard, or narrative noise.
- `/burp` and short-horizon expansion are weaker than the earlier hot period.
- Fresh Pump names exist, but clean follow-through is less consistent.

Preferred behavior:

- Stay selective.
- Prefer WATCH over ENTER for marginal setups.
- Require clearer lane fit.
- Let no-trade sessions happen naturally.

What not to do:

- Do not widen gates just because people are active in chat.
- Do not force trades to match social excitement.

## Weak Or Dead Tape

Signs:

- Few meaningful runners.
- Weak short-horizon expansion.
- Plenty of launches, little durable continuation.
- Chatter recycles old names more than it discovers new clean ones.

Preferred behavior:

- Be highly skeptical.
- Treat no trade as the default acceptable outcome.
- Demand unusually strong structure before ENTER.

What not to do:

- Do not try to manufacture action.
- Do not let narrative or one-off wallet touches override weak structure.

## How To Use Regime Context

- Regime context should shape confidence, not rewrite hard rules.
- Hot tape can justify slightly more confidence in already-strong setups.
- Mixed tape should increase caution on marginal setups.
- Weak tape should make WATCH and REJECT more common.

## Saturday-Night Lesson

- Active Telegram chatter does not necessarily mean a hot runner environment.
- Saturday night showed:
  - strong room activity, especially in Yakuza
  - real discussion and live management of names
  - but only a mixed runner environment from Rick
- In that regime, repeated `0`-trade sessions were healthy behavior.
