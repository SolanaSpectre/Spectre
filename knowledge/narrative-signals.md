# Narrative Signals

Narrative context helps explain flow, but narrative is not the same thing as edge.

## Useful Narrative Inputs

- Telegram chatter density.
- Repeated CA or ticker mentions.
- Twitter spread and repeated mentions by different accounts.
- Platform or ecosystem updates that change behavior in the memecoin market.

## What Good Narrative Looks Like

- Multiple independent mentions over a short window.
- Consistent story across sources.
- Narrative that supports observed on-chain flow rather than replacing it.
- Persistent holder behavior, where people keep posting and holding through time instead of only during the first burst.

## Holder-Culture Signal

- Some strong memecoin moves are driven by sticky holders, not just fast chart momentum.
- A stronger community signal appears when people:
  - keep posting through volatility
  - hold overnight instead of reflex-dumping
  - wake up still aligned with the coin and its culture
- "People still here tomorrow" is a stronger signal than "people loud right now."

## What Bad Narrative Looks Like

- Spam repetition without real trade quality.
- One loud account with no supporting flow.
- Hype that conflicts with weak liquidity or poor route quality.
- Empty cult posting with no real holder persistence or structural support.

## Rule

- Narrative can increase or decrease confidence.
- Narrative should not overrule hard structural risk.
- Use narrative as context, not as a substitute for market mechanics.
- Durable community persistence is useful only when it agrees with acceptable liquidity and structure.
